🥗 Paneer Butter Masala (Vegetarian Classic)

Description: A high-impact, crowd-tested North Indian dish. Creamy, mildly spiced, and operationally simple once prepped.

Ingredients:

Core Components

  • Paneer (cubed)
  • 2 medium tomatoes(pureed)
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste

Flavor & Spice Layer

  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt (to taste)

Fat & Texture Agents

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream
  • 1 tbsp oil

Instructions:

  1. Base Setup
    • Heat oil + 1 tbsp butter in a pan
    • Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown
    • Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until raw smell dissipates
  2. Tomato Integration
    • Add tomato puree
    • Cook on medium flame until oil separates (critical indicator of readiness)
  3. Spice Injection
    • Add turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, salt
    • Mix thoroughly and cook for ~2-3 minutes
  4. Texture Development
    • Add ½ cup water and stir to form gravy
    • Introduce paneer cubes and simmer for 5 minutes
  5. Final Enrichment
    • Add cream + remaining butter
    • Sprinkle garam masala
    • Optional: add sugar for balance
  6. Finish & Serve
    • Garnish with coriander leaves
    • Serve hot with roti, naan, or rice
